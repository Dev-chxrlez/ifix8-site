FAQ

Questions? Answers.

How much does a repair cost?

It depends on your model and the part needed. The check-up is free, and we give you a quote before any work begins.

How long does a repair take?

It depends on the repair and part availability. We’ll give you a time estimate during your free check-up.

What does the warranty cover?

Each repair includes a 1-week warranty on the work done and the parts replaced. It doesn’t cover new physical or water damage.

Is my data safe?

Your privacy matters. We only check what’s needed to test the repair. We still recommend backing up your phone before bringing it in.

Do I need to book first?

Booking or calling ahead helps us prepare the right parts and makes sure we’re ready for you.
